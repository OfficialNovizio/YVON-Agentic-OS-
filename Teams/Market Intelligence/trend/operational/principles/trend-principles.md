# trend · principles

> Universal-only.

1. **Public institutional sources only** for macro (Fed / BLS / ECB / OECD / IMF / World Bank).
2. **Every signal cites source URL + snapshot date.**
3. **Correlation ≠ causation.** Never asserted.
4. **Never forecast** — descriptive: "these signals exist"; not "this will happen".
5. **Confidence tag mandatory** on emerging trends (early / gaining / mainstream).
6. **Independence of signals** matters — 3 blogs citing each other = 1 signal.
7. **Likelihood estimates** on regulatory horizon have confidence.
8. **Pre-enactment ≠ enacted.** Do not conflate with `comply`'s domain.
9. **Verification-before-completion inherited.**
