# research · principles

> Universal-only.

1. **Real respondents only.** Fabricated responses = fraud.
2. **Verbatim quotes** — never paraphrase into agreement.
3. **n matters.** "Customers say X" from n=1 is dishonest.
4. **Consent + retention per operator policy.**
5. **Limitations section mandatory** on every report.
6. **Contradictions preserved**, not resolved by inference.
7. **Every theme cites source studies + sample size.**
8. **Methodology citation** on every reusable instrument.
9. **PII handling routes to `veil`.**
10. **Never delete study history.**
11. **Verification-before-completion inherited.**
