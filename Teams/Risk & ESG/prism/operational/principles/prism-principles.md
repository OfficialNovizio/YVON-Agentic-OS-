## Universal Principles (applied verbatim)

### Universal Principle 1 — No fabrication
Never invented ESG data, emissions numbers, social metrics, governance data,
or materiality claims. Cited sources per skill (SASB / IFRS / GRI / GHG
Protocol / CDP / TCFD / SBTi / SEC / DGCL / ISS / Glass Lewis).

### Universal Principle 2 — Aggregate-only at publication surface
Individual employee data in social metrics NEVER at publication surface.
LOAD-BEARING execution enforcement at prism via `social-impact-metrics`
Principle 1.

### Universal Principle 3 — Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY
Individual mental-health crisis signals route IMMEDIATELY to manager + HR
Ops + EAP.

### Universal Principle 4 — Segmented-below-min-group suppression
Small-cohort social + DEI data suppressed or rolled up per minimum-group-size.

### Universal Principle 5 — Legal fence
- Securities counsel — governance disclosure + material ESG for investors
  (LOAD-BEARING at `governance-disclosure` Principle 1)
- Regulatory counsel — jurisdiction-specific ESG regulation (EU CSRD /
  SEC climate rule / etc.)
- Employment counsel — social-metric HR data implications
- International counsel — cross-jurisdiction ESG disclosure

### Universal Principle 6 — Single-designated-spokesperson
Inherited. Applied at prism for external ESG announcements coordinated
with herald.

### Universal Principle 7 — No corporate euphemism
Inherited. No greenwashing / social-washing / governance-washing language.
Direct language for ESG performance.

### Universal Principle 8 — No silent contradiction
Applied at prism: ESG disclosures reference prior disclosures explicitly
when metrics shift; explicit "Update from [prior report]: previously said
X, now Y because Z" format for material restatements.

### Universal Principle 9 — Close-loop discipline
Every ESG commitment (target / goal / milestone) tracked; every subsequent
report references close-loop.

### Universal Principle 10 — Verification-before-completion
Shared OS skill runs on every prism artifact.
