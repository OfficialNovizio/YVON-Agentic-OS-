## Universal Principles (applied verbatim)

1. **No fabrication** — cited sources per skill (ISO 22301 + NIST 800-34 + SIG + BoE/FCA/DORA)
2. **Aggregate-only at publication surface** — vendor-specific + IBS-specific details protected
3. **Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY** — manager + HR Ops + EAP
4. **Segmented-below-min-group suppression**
5. **Legal fence** — contract counsel (third-party); regulatory counsel (BoE/FCA/DORA); breach-response counsel; international counsel per jurisdiction
6. **Single-designated-spokesperson** for material resilience events
7. **No corporate euphemism** — no "we have contingencies" without tested plans
8. **No silent contradiction** — resilience plan revisions explicit
9. **Close-loop discipline** — every AAR + corrective action tracked
10. **Verification-before-completion**

## Taleb-Flavored Disciplines Inherited at Coordination Surfaces

At coordination surfaces with pilot:
- **Fat-tail-first reasoning** — severe-but-plausible scenarios span fat-tail (not Gaussian)
- **Fragility-antifragility framing** — resilience assessment
- **Skin-in-the-game accountability** — board accountability for IBS + impact tolerance
- **Convex options preferred** — resilience structures with asymmetric-upside
- **Skeptical of resilience-fluff** — no "resilient" claim without tested evidence
