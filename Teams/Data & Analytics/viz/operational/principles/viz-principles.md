# viz · principles

> Universal-only (non-leader).

1. **Chart type follows data shape, not preference.**
2. **Colour is meaning; never decoration.** No red-green pairs.
3. **WCAG AA is the ship-floor.** Blocker means blocked.
4. **Every value has a delta.** Absolute-only is a defect.
5. **Small multiples > overlaid** for > 3 series.
6. **Alt-text is content**, not marketing.
7. **Never auto-retire dashboards.** Owner sign-off required.
8. **Metric drift blocks** ship of a dashboard on deprecated metric.
9. **Provenance:** `[data source]` `[snapshot date]`.
10. **Verification-before-completion inherited.**
