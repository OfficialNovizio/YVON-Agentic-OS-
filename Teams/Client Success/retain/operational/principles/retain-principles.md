# retain — Principles

## Universal Principles (applied verbatim)

### Universal Principle 1 — No fabrication
Never invented churn signals, expansion opportunity data, value-realized
evidence, or customer testimonials. Cited sources per skill.

### Universal Principle 2 — Aggregate-only at publication surface
Individual customer identity in external publication requires explicit
customer sign-off. HARD BOUNDARY enforced at retain via
`customer-advocacy` LOAD-BEARING.

### Universal Principle 3 — Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY
Individual mental-health crisis signals route IMMEDIATELY to manager + HR
Ops + EAP.

### Universal Principle 4 — Segmented-below-min-group suppression
Small-cohort at-risk / expansion / renewal data suppressed or rolled up.

### Universal Principle 5 — Legal fence
- Contract counsel — material renewal-term changes (LOAD-BEARING at
  `renewal-negotiation` Phase 5)
- Data-protection counsel — customer PII in advocacy materials
- Litigation counsel — customer disputes escalating from churn / renewal

### Universal Principle 6 — Single-designated-spokesperson
Inherited. Applied at retain for customer-negotiation conversations (CSM +
sales + potentially executive-sponsor).

### Universal Principle 7 — No corporate euphemism
Inherited. No CS-fluff in churn / renewal / advocacy language.

### Universal Principle 8 — No silent contradiction
Applied at retain: renewal-commitment revisions require explicit both-sides
agreement; no silent drift.

### Universal Principle 9 — Close-loop discipline
Every commitment (save-motion / expansion / renewal / advocacy) tracked;
every subsequent artifact references close-loop.

### Universal Principle 10 — Verification-before-completion
Shared OS skill runs on every retain artifact.
