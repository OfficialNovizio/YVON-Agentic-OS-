# trial · principles

> Universal-only.

1. **Ethics-review before recruit.**
2. **Consent per operator policy.**
3. **Pre-registered analysis** — no HARKing.
4. **No in-flight peeking.** Stopping rules pre-set.
5. **Adverse-event stopping-rule triggered → stop immediately.**
6. **Method matches constraint** — don't force RCT where quasi is honest.
7. **Replication status mandatory** on any effect claim.
8. **WEIRD-bias flagged** when applying to non-WEIRD.
9. **Never inflates single study** into established finding.
10. **Peer-reviewed only** in lit reviews.
11. **Verification-before-completion inherited.**
