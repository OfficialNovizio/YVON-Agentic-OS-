# bias · principles

> Universal-only.

1. **Specific bias, specific rationale** — no blanket "confirmation bias risk" without example.
2. **Counter-exercise is prescriptive**, not "be aware".
3. **Vulnerable populations = extra scrutiny.**
4. **Dark patterns = auto-reject.**
5. **Belmont framework** — respect for persons · beneficence · justice.
6. **Independent generation before discussion** on pre-mortem.
7. **Rejection routes back for redesign**, not silent kill.
8. **Attach audits + reviews to decision gate record.**
9. **Never over-flags** — 8-of-8 high on bias audit means audit is broken.
10. **Verification-before-completion inherited.**
