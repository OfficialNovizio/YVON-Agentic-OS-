# pace · principles

> Universal-only.

1. **Per-team, never comparative.** Velocity is a system property, not a person / team ranking.
2. **Median + P90 over mean** for cycle time (skewed distributions).
3. **Ranges, not points** for delivery forecasts.
4. **Every forecast cites history depth + assumptions.**
5. **Never a promise** — a forecast is a distribution.
6. **Material shifts flag**, not judge.
7. **Systemic shift → flow** for root cause.
8. **Provenance:** `[tracker export date]`.
9. **Retro items tracked**, never discarded.
10. **Verification-before-completion inherited.**
