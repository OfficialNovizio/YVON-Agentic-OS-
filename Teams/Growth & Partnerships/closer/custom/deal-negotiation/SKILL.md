# Deal Negotiation

## Introduction

Deal-negotiation framework for closer — Fisher & Ury BATNA + principled
negotiation + Voss tactical empathy (Never Split the Difference) + Malhotra/
Bazerman Negotiation Genius + Winning by Design SaaS-specific.

Custom Route D per §8.2.

## Purpose

Prevents seven failure modes:

1. **Closing without discovery complete.** Attempting close before customer
   pain + fit + decision criteria understood = high loss rate + wasted cycles.
   LOAD-BEARING per Principle 1.
2. **Concessions without exchange.** Giving pricing / term concessions without
   corresponding customer commitments = value leaked + weakens future
   negotiations. LOAD-BEARING per Principle 2 (inherited from retain renewal-
   negotiation pattern).
3. **BATNA blindness.** Not knowing customer's alternatives = weak position.
4. **Position-driven negotiation.** Fighting over stated positions instead of
   underlying interests = zero-sum + relationship damage.
5. **Aggressive tactics inappropriate for context** — high-pressure closing
   for consultative-sale = trust damage + churn risk if won.
6. **Material term concession without counsel** — legal exposure.
7. **Individual crisis DURING negotiation-crunch.** HARD BOUNDARY.

## Structure / Protocol

```
FISHER & URY PRINCIPLED NEGOTIATION (4 principles)

  1. Separate people from problem — relationship preservation
  2. Focus on interests, not positions — win-win discovery
  3. Invent options for mutual gain — creative solutions
  4. Insist on objective criteria — benchmarks / precedent


VOSS TACTICAL EMPATHY (Never Split the Difference)

  - Mirroring — repeat last 3 words to encourage elaboration
  - Labeling — "It seems like..." to acknowledge emotion
  - Calibrated questions — "How am I supposed to do that?" reframes
  - "No" as starting point, not end
  - Accusation audit — preempt objections


BATNA DISCIPLINE

  Our BATNA — what happens if this deal doesn't close (pipeline / capacity
  redeployment / market position)
  Customer BATNA — competitor / build-in-house / do-nothing


CONCESSION-EXCHANGE FRAMEWORK

  Never give without ask. Standard exchanges:
    - Discount → multi-year commitment
    - Payment terms → up-front payment
    - Feature customization → volume commitment
    - Free trial → conversion timeline
    - Reference willingness → discount / feature access


OPERATIONAL SEQUENCE:

  Phase 1: DISCOVERY-COMPLETE VERIFICATION (LOAD-BEARING)  (pain + fit + decision criteria)
  Phase 2: BATNA + INTEREST ANALYSIS                        (both sides)
  Phase 3: NEGOTIATION STRATEGY DESIGN                       (principled + tactical empathy)
  Phase 4: CONCESSION-EXCHANGE PLANNING (LOAD-BEARING)       (never give without ask)
  Phase 5: CLOSE + COUNSEL-REVIEW MATERIAL TERMS             (operator + counsel per Universal Principle 5)
```

## Instructions

### Phase 1 — Discovery-complete verification (LOAD-BEARING)
Verify customer discovery complete per `customer-discovery` sibling:
- Pain identified + quantified
- Fit validated
- Decision criteria understood
- Champion + economic buyer identified

If discovery incomplete → return to discovery. LOAD-BEARING per Principle 1.

### Phase 2 — BATNA + interest analysis
- Our BATNA articulated internally
- Customer BATNA understood
- Underlying interests (both sides) identified vs stated positions

### Phase 3 — Negotiation strategy design
- Fisher & Ury 4 principles applied
- Voss tactical empathy techniques planned
- Objective criteria (benchmarks / precedent) prepared

### Phase 4 — Concession-exchange planning (LOAD-BEARING)
- Never-give-without-ask discipline per Principle 2
- Concession-exchange table prepared

### Phase 5 — Close + counsel-review material terms
- Material terms → operator + counsel (Universal Principle 5)
- Close + contract execution → operator + counsel + CFO

## Output Format

- Discovery-completeness verification memo
- BATNA + interest analysis (both sides)
- Negotiation strategy playbook per deal
- Concession-exchange plan
- Counsel-review handoff for material terms

## Principles

1. **Never closing without discovery complete** — LOAD-BEARING per failure mode 1.
2. **Never concessions without exchange** — LOAD-BEARING per failure mode 2.
3. **Principled negotiation over positions.**
4. **BATNA discipline both sides.**
5. **Material term counsel-reviewed** — Universal Principle 5 inherited.
6. **Tactical empathy** — Voss techniques for high-stakes / difficult
   negotiations.
7. **No fabrication** — cited sources. Universal Principle 1.
8. **Aggregate-only at publication surface** — Universal Principle 2.
9. **Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY** — Universal Principle 3 inherited.
10. **§0.6 flag.** Sources Tier B.

## Fallback

- **Discovery-incomplete pressure to close** — decline per Principle 1.
  Return to discovery.
- **Concession-request without exchange offer** — decline per Principle 2.
  Propose exchange.
- **Material term negotiation without counsel** — escalate per Principle 5.
- **Aggressive-tactic pressure for consultative-sale** — decline; damage
  outweighs win.
- **Individual crisis signal.** STOP. Route per Universal Principle 3.
