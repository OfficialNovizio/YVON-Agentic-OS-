# Customer Discovery

## Introduction

Customer discovery framework for closer — Blank/Cooper Customer Development +
Bosworth Solution Selling + Ulwick Jobs-to-be-Done + Keenan Gap Selling +
Rackham SPIN Selling.

Custom Route D per §8.2.

## Purpose

Prevents six failure modes:

1. **Leading questions that manufacture demand.** Sales-rep questions
   engineered to elicit specific pain / need = fabricated qualification +
   false-positive pipeline. LOAD-BEARING per Principle 1.
2. **Shallow discovery.** Pain identified without root-cause + impact
   quantification = weak business case + high loss rate.
3. **Wrong-methodology for context.** SPIN for informed enterprise buyer =
   under-value; Jobs-to-be-Done for tactical purchase = over-analysis.
4. **Single-stakeholder discovery.** Discovery only with champion misses
   economic buyer / users / blockers per Miller Heiman discipline.
5. **Discovery-as-pitch.** Rep talking instead of listening = no learning +
   customer disengagement.
6. **Individual crisis DURING discovery-crunch.** HARD BOUNDARY.

## Structure / Protocol

```
DISCOVERY METHODOLOGIES

  CUSTOMER DEVELOPMENT (Blank) — pre-PMF discovery
    - Customer discovery → validation → creation → building
    - Best for: new markets / novel product

  SOLUTION SELLING (Bosworth) — pain-driven consultative
    - Diagnose pain → propose solution
    - Best for: solution-fit-obvious once pain diagnosed

  JOBS-TO-BE-DONE (Ulwick) — outcome-driven
    - What job is customer "hiring" product to do?
    - Best for: outcome-focused positioning + differentiation

  GAP SELLING (Keenan) — current/future state gap
    - Current / Future / Gap / Impact / Root cause
    - Best for: customer unclear on gap at start

  SPIN SELLING (Rackham) — question-driven
    - Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-payoff
    - Foundational for consultative-selling


DISCOVERY DISCIPLINE

  - Open questions > closed
  - Listening > talking (target 70/30 customer/rep)
  - Multi-stakeholder (champion + economic buyer + users + blockers)
  - Root-cause depth (Toyota "5 Whys")
  - Impact quantification (business consequence of unresolved)


LEADING-QUESTION AVOIDANCE (LOAD-BEARING)

  Leading: "Doesn't the slow report generation frustrate your team?"
  Non-leading: "Walk me through how your team currently handles reporting."

  Leading questions manufacture desired-answers = fabricated qualification.


OPERATIONAL SEQUENCE:

  Phase 1: METHODOLOGY SELECTION PER CONTEXT
  Phase 2: DISCOVERY INTERVIEW DESIGN                       (open + non-leading)
  Phase 3: MULTI-STAKEHOLDER DISCOVERY                      (champion + economic + users + blockers)
  Phase 4: ROOT-CAUSE + IMPACT QUANTIFICATION               (business consequence)
  Phase 5: DISCOVERY-COMPLETE VERIFICATION FOR CLOSE HANDOFF (feeds deal-negotiation gate)
```

## Instructions

### Phase 1 — Methodology selection per context
Match methodology to context.

### Phase 2 — Discovery interview design
- Open + non-leading questions
- Listening discipline (70/30)
- Sequenced from broad to specific

### Phase 3 — Multi-stakeholder discovery
- Champion + economic buyer + users + blockers
- Per-stakeholder discovery adapted

### Phase 4 — Root-cause + impact quantification
- Root-cause depth ("5 Whys")
- Impact quantification (business consequence)

### Phase 5 — Discovery-complete verification for close handoff
- Pain identified + quantified
- Fit validated
- Decision criteria understood
- Champion + economic buyer identified

Feeds `deal-negotiation` Phase 1 LOAD-BEARING gate.

## Output Format

- Methodology selection memo
- Discovery interview design
- Multi-stakeholder discovery plan
- Root-cause + impact quantification report
- Discovery-completeness memo for close handoff

## Principles

1. **Never leading questions that manufacture demand** — LOAD-BEARING per
   failure mode 1.
2. **Open-question discipline** — no closed-question interrogation.
3. **Listening > talking** — 70/30 target.
4. **Multi-stakeholder mandatory** — champion + economic + users + blockers.
5. **Root-cause depth** — "5 Whys" or equivalent.
6. **Impact quantification** — business consequence explicit.
7. **No fabrication** — cited sources. Universal Principle 1.
8. **Aggregate-only at publication surface** — Universal Principle 2. Individual
   customer discovery data stays in CRM.
9. **Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY** — Universal Principle 3 inherited.
10. **§0.6 flag.** Sources Tier B.

## Fallback

- **Stakeholder access constrained** (only champion available). Escalate to
  champion for economic-buyer + user access; if not possible, flag deal risk.
- **Root-cause unclear** — return to discovery; do NOT jump to solution.
- **Impact quantification challenge** — coordinate with customer for data;
  do NOT fabricate business case.
- **Individual crisis signal.** STOP. Route per Universal Principle 3.
