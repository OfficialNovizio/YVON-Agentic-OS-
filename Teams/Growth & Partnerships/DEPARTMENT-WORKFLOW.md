# Growth & Partnerships — Department Workflow

> **quest** (Growth & Partnerships Lead — Mark Roberge identity) sequences
> multi-agent G&P work.

## Sequencing Principle

Multi-agent tasks route to quest who sequences closer, lure, bond per
Roberge-flavored disciplines.

## Standard Sequencing Patterns

### Pattern A — Full GTM Launch (all 4 agents + cross-department)

**Trigger:** new product / feature / market launch.

**quest sequences:**

1. **quest `growth-strategy`** — stage-appropriate framework + revenue-
   machine audit + growth-model recommendation
2. **quest `pricing-and-packaging`** — LOAD-BEARING WTP + operator/CFO/counsel handoff
3. **quest `gtm-motion-selection`** — LOAD-BEARING customer-segment analysis + motion design
4. **quest `funnel-metrics-and-attribution`** — instrumentation coordination with dana + Product
5. **PARALLEL:**
   - **closer `sales-methodology-and-playbook`** — playbook per motion
   - **lure `demand-generation-strategy`** — demand-gen mix per motion
   - **bond `partner-selection-and-tiering`** — partnership motion if scale-stage
6. **PARALLEL execution:**
   - closer + lure MQL-SQL alignment
   - lure + bond co-marketing coordination
   - closer + bond deal-registration + co-selling
7. **Sales-to-CS handoff** at deal close → kickoff (Client Success)
8. **Post-launch measurement** → quest `funnel-metrics-and-attribution`
9. **Material growth events** → beacon `investor-cadence` (Comms & PR)

### Pattern B — Enterprise Deal (closer-led with quest coordination)

**Trigger:** enterprise-tier deal in pipeline.

**quest sequences:**

1. **closer `customer-discovery`** — Multi-stakeholder discovery with Miller
   Heiman
2. **closer `sales-methodology-and-playbook`** — MEDDIC / Challenger applied
3. **closer `pipeline-management`** — stage discipline
4. **closer `deal-negotiation`** — LOAD-BEARING discovery-completeness gate
   + BATNA + concession-exchange
5. **Material terms** → operator + CFO + counsel (LOAD-BEARING per Universal
   Principle 5)
6. **Close** → kickoff `kickoff-executive-alignment` (Client Success —
   MSP handoff)

### Pattern C — Marketing Campaign (lure-led with cross-dept coordination)

**Trigger:** new marketing campaign / demand-gen initiative.

**quest sequences:**

1. **lure `demand-generation-strategy`** — LOAD-BEARING attribution
   instrumentation
2. **lure `content-marketing-funnel`** — LOAD-BEARING factual grounding
3. **lure `marketing-attribution-and-mtx`** — instrumentation execution
4. **lure `account-based-marketing`** (if ABM motion) — LOAD-BEARING data
   compliance
5. **Coordinate with Brand Studio** for creative + channel execution
6. **Coordinate with closer** for MQL-SQL alignment
7. **Coordinate with bond** if co-marketing dimension

### Pattern D — New Partnership (bond-led with quest + closer coordination)

**Trigger:** partnership initiative from strategic need.

**quest sequences:**

1. **bond `partner-selection-and-tiering`** — LOAD-BEARING counsel-scoped
   agreement
2. **bond `channel-partner-program`** — LOAD-BEARING revenue-share with counsel
3. **bond `co-marketing-and-co-selling`** — LOAD-BEARING both-parties sign-off
4. **bond `strategic-alliance-management`** (if deep alliance) — LOAD-BEARING
   conflict-of-interest counsel review
5. **Board approval** for major alliances
6. **Material alliance investor comms** → beacon
