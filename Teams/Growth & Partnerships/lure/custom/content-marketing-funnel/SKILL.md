# Content Marketing Funnel

## Introduction

Content marketing funnel discipline for lure — Pulizzi Epic Content Marketing
+ Handley Everybody Writes + Content Marketing Institute research +
MarketingProfs practitioner.

**Scope distinction:** lure OWNS content-marketing strategy + editorial
planning + topic architecture. Brand Studio (lena / weave / muse) execute
content creation. Product provides subject-matter expertise.

Custom Route D per §8.2.

## Purpose

Prevents six failure modes:

1. **Content-marketing without factual grounding.** Published content with
   fabricated statistics / attributions / claims = credibility damage +
   potential defamation. LOAD-BEARING per Principle 1.
2. **Random-act-of-content.** Content without editorial planning = drift +
   no funnel coherence.
3. **Funnel-stage-agnostic content.** Same tone/depth for awareness vs
   consideration vs decision = wrong content per stage.
4. **Topic-cluster ignorance.** Modern SEO requires topic-authority via
   cluster architecture; individual-post approach = weak organic performance.
5. **Content-marketing without measurement.** Publishing without measurement =
   can't optimize.
6. **Individual crisis DURING content crunch.** HARD BOUNDARY.

## Structure / Protocol

```
FUNNEL-STAGE CONTENT (canonical AARRR-adjacent)

  AWARENESS
    - Educational content (thought leadership, industry analysis)
    - Broad topic-cluster content
    - SEO-optimized long-form
    - No product pitch

  CONSIDERATION
    - Comparison content
    - Solution-oriented content
    - Buyer-guide content
    - Product-adjacent but not pitch-heavy

  DECISION
    - Case studies (coordinate with retain customer-advocacy for sign-off)
    - Product-demo content
    - Pricing / ROI content
    - Testimonials (with sign-off per Universal Principle 2)

  ADVOCACY / RETENTION
    - Customer-success stories
    - Advanced tutorials
    - Community content (with retain customer-advocacy coordination)


TOPIC-CLUSTER ARCHITECTURE (modern SEO)

  Pillar page (broad topic — 3000+ words)
   ├── Cluster content (specific subtopics — 1500-2500 words each)
   ├── Cluster content
   └── Cluster content (all internally linked to pillar)

  Coordinate with rank (Engineering SEO) for technical implementation.


FACTUAL GROUNDING DISCIPLINE (LOAD-BEARING)

  Every claim / statistic / attribution cited:
    - Sourced from public institutional research
    - OR cited to specific practitioner with attribution
    - OR clearly flagged as "internal data — assumption X"

  Fabricated statistics + wrong attributions = credibility damage +
  potential defamation risk (coordinate with counsel).


OPERATIONAL SEQUENCE:

  Phase 1: CONTENT AUDIT + EDITORIAL PLANNING
  Phase 2: TOPIC-CLUSTER ARCHITECTURE (SEO)
  Phase 3: FUNNEL-STAGE CONTENT DESIGN
  Phase 4: FACTUAL GROUNDING DISCIPLINE (LOAD-BEARING)
  Phase 5: MEASUREMENT + OPTIMIZATION
```

## Instructions

### Phase 1 — Content audit + editorial planning
Audit existing content; plan editorial calendar per funnel stage per topic cluster.

### Phase 2 — Topic-cluster architecture
Design pillar + cluster structure per major topic area. Coordinate with rank
(Engineering SEO) for technical implementation.

### Phase 3 — Funnel-stage content design
Match content type + tone + depth to funnel stage.

### Phase 4 — Factual grounding discipline (LOAD-BEARING)
Every published claim cited or explicitly flagged as internal-estimate.

### Phase 5 — Measurement + optimization
Content-performance metrics (traffic / conversion / engagement) + optimization.

## Output Format

- Content audit report
- Editorial calendar per funnel stage + topic cluster
- Topic-cluster architecture design
- Content briefs (handoff to Brand Studio)
- Factual-grounding gate for pre-publish review
- Content performance dashboard

## Principles

1. **Never content-marketing without factual grounding** — LOAD-BEARING per
   failure mode 1.
2. **Editorial planning mandatory** — no random content.
3. **Funnel-stage-appropriate** content.
4. **Topic-cluster architecture** for modern SEO.
5. **Every published claim cited** — no fabricated statistics.
6. **No fabrication** — Universal Principle 1.
7. **Aggregate-only at publication surface** — Universal Principle 2. Case
   study customer identity with sign-off (coordinate with retain).
8. **Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY** — Universal Principle 3 inherited.
9. **§0.6 flag.** Sources Tier B.

## Fallback

- **Statistic without source** for pre-publish content — reject per Principle
  1 + 5. Coordinate research or flag as internal-estimate.
- **Case-study without customer sign-off** — coordinate with retain
  `customer-advocacy` for sign-off before publish.
- **Individual crisis signal.** STOP. Route per Universal Principle 3.
