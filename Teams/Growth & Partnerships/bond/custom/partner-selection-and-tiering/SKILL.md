# Partner Selection and Tiering

## Introduction

Partner selection + tiering discipline for bond — Rangan MIT + Doz & Hamel
Alliance Advantage + Kotler + Impartner/Allbound PRM + Forrester research.

Custom Route D per §8.2.

## Purpose

Prevents six failure modes:

1. **Partner engagement without counsel-scoped agreement.** Verbal / handshake
   partnerships without legal structure = disputes + IP leakage + revenue
   confusion. LOAD-BEARING per Principle 1 (Universal Principle 5 inherited).
2. **Undifferentiated tier structure.** All partners treated equally = no
   incentive for high-value partners to invest.
3. **Partner-need ambiguity.** Signing partners without clear business need =
   partnership drift.
4. **Cultural / strategic mismatch.** Partners misaligned with strategy =
   friction + wasted management overhead.
5. **Exclusivity without evaluation** — exclusive agreements without
   competitive-landscape review = future flexibility loss.
6. **Individual crisis DURING partnership crunch.** HARD BOUNDARY.

## Structure / Protocol

```
PARTNER TYPES

  CHANNEL / RESELLER — sells product to end customers
  TECHNOLOGY / INTEGRATION — product integrates with our product
  REFERRAL — refers customers without transacting
  IMPLEMENTATION / SERVICES — implements product for customers
  DISTRIBUTION — physical / geographic distribution
  STRATEGIC ALLIANCE — deep co-development / co-market (see strategic-alliance-management)


TIER FRAMEWORK (Rangan + Impartner/Allbound)

  PLATINUM / STRATEGIC — top partners; deep investment both sides
  GOLD — high-performing; program-defined benefits
  SILVER — mid-tier; standard program
  BRONZE / REGISTERED — entry-level; self-service


SELECTION CRITERIA

  - Strategic fit (customer base overlap / complementary capability)
  - Cultural fit (values / working style / commitment level)
  - Financial stability
  - Track record (references from existing partners)
  - Exclusivity implications (if applicable)
  - Legal / compliance posture


OPERATIONAL SEQUENCE:

  Phase 1: PARTNER-NEED IDENTIFICATION
  Phase 2: CANDIDATE ASSESSMENT
  Phase 3: TIER ASSIGNMENT
  Phase 4: SELECTION + LOAD-BEARING COUNSEL-SCOPED AGREEMENT
```

## Instructions

### Phase 1 — Partner-need identification
Clear business need per partnership type.

### Phase 2 — Candidate assessment
Multi-criteria evaluation (strategic + cultural + financial + track record).

### Phase 3 — Tier assignment
Match candidate to program tier per criteria + expected investment.

### Phase 4 — Selection + counsel-scoped agreement (LOAD-BEARING)
- Partner agreement drafted by operator + counsel
- Revenue share / IP terms / exclusivity / termination clauses
- **No partnership engagement without signed counsel-scoped agreement**

## Output Format

- Partner-need memo per business need
- Candidate assessment matrix
- Tier assignment recommendation
- Counsel-scoping brief for agreement

## Principles

1. **Never partner engagement without counsel-scoped agreement** — LOAD-BEARING
   per failure mode 1.
2. **Differentiated tier structure** — no undifferentiated all-partners approach.
3. **Clear partner-need before selection.**
4. **Multi-criteria evaluation** — strategic + cultural + financial + track record.
5. **Exclusivity evaluated** with competitive-landscape review.
6. **No fabrication** — cited sources. Universal Principle 1.
7. **Aggregate-only at publication surface** — Universal Principle 2. Partner
   commercial terms stay in operator + counsel + procurement tools.
8. **Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY** — Universal Principle 3 inherited.
9. **§0.6 flag.** Sources Tier B.

## Fallback

- **Partnership pressure without counsel agreement** — decline per Principle 1.
- **Cultural / strategic mismatch discovered late** — coordinate exit/restructure
  with operator + counsel.
- **Individual crisis signal.** STOP. Route per Universal Principle 3.
