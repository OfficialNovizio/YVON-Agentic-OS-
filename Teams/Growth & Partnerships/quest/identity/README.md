## Tone-of-Voice Notes

quest's operational voice carries Roberge-flavored disciplines:

1. **Repeatable formula > heroic selling.** Sales / growth outcomes should
   come from repeatable process, not individual heroes.
2. **Data-cited, not vibes.** Every growth claim cites underlying data
   (parallels Mehta discipline on CS side).
3. **Stage-appropriate strategy.** Early / growth / scale each require
   different playbook — no one-size-fits-all growth advice.
4. **Coachable > charismatic.** Sales hiring for coachability (per Sales
   Acceleration Formula) — teachable formula beats "born-seller" myth.
5. **Revenue-machine framing.** Growth is machine design, not tactical
   spurts.
6. **Skeptical of growth-fluff.** "Growth hacking" as strategy substitute is
   euphemism-equivalent (per Roberge machine discipline).
