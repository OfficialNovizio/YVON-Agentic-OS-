# Expansion Portfolio Management

## Introduction

This skill packages multi-market portfolio management discipline for compass —
for orgs operating in ≥3 international markets. Portfolio inventory + market
classification (Ghemawat-adjusted BCG matrix with CAGE-adjusted metrics) +
cross-market learning transfer + rebalancing decisions (double-down / hold /
divest per market) + market-exit protocol coordination.

**Scope distinction:** this is MULTI-MARKET rebalancing — orgs already in
≥3 markets deciding which markets to grow, hold, or exit. Distinct from
`market-selection-framework` (Phase 1 — first / next market selection),
`entry-mode-decision` (Phase 2 — mode for a specific new market), and
`go-to-market-adaptation` (Phase 3 — GTM for a specific market).

Reclassified from a marketplace scope-mismatch per §4.6 — the "portfolio
management" marketplace skills are all financial-portfolio (stocks/ETFs/
wealth-mgmt/FX for portfolios), completely different domain. Business-market
portfolio management is a distinct discipline anchored on Ghemawat +
Rugman & Verbeke.

Custom Route D per §8.2 — cited rubric grounded in Ghemawat's canonical
corpus + Rugman & Verbeke regional-vs-global + Porter + BCG + MGI.

## Purpose

Prevents six failure modes that show up when multi-market portfolio is
unmanaged:

1. **Portfolio drift without periodic review.** No annual portfolio review =
   markets that should have been divested continue consuming resources; markets
   that should have been doubled-down get under-resourced. Silent drift is the
   default failure mode.
2. **Financial-portfolio-only view.** Applying only financial metrics (revenue,
   margin) to market decisions ignores strategic value (market-learning /
   brand-presence / talent access) + Ghemawat CAGE-adjusted comparables.
3. **Cross-market learning discarded.** What worked in market X often applies
   to markets Y, Z with similar CAGE profile. Failure to transfer learning =
   repeated first-market mistakes across the portfolio.
4. **Regional-vs-global strategy blindness.** Rugman & Verbeke's insight: most
   MNEs are REGIONAL not global. Portfolio decisions ignoring regional
   clustering underestimate the value of regional density + overestimate
   scattered-global reach.
5. **Divest decision without proper exit protocol.** Market-exit involves WARN
   Act equivalents (varies by jurisdiction — France requires social plan,
   Germany requires works-council consultation, US requires WARN Act 60-day
   notice for ≥100 employees), customer-transition handling, brand-reputation
   impact, and investor comms. Fast-exit without protocol = predictable
   employment-law violation + reputational damage.
6. **Individual crisis during portfolio-review conversation.** Team members
   under portfolio-pressure + personal distress can coincide. HARD BOUNDARY
   per Universal Principle 3.

compass uses this skill as Phase 4+ of expansion strategy — activated once
the org operates in ≥3 international markets. Coordinates with all 3 compass
siblings + canopy + hire + signal + beacon per event.

## Structure / Protocol

The portfolio-mgmt workflow combines Ghemawat-adjusted BCG + cross-market
learning + regional clustering + exit protocol:

```
GHEMAWAT-ADJUSTED BCG MATRIX (BCG classic × Ghemawat CAGE adjustment)

  Classic BCG (Boston Consulting Group):
    - Star           high market share × high market growth
    - Cash Cow       high market share × low market growth
    - Question Mark  low market share × high market growth
    - Dog            low market share × low market growth

  Ghemawat-adjustment for market portfolio (not product portfolio):
    - "Market share" replaced with organizational-position-in-market
      (revenue rank / customer count / brand recognition per market)
    - "Market growth" adjusted by CAGE-distance factor (a high-growth market
      with high CAGE distance may not be reachable at expected pace)
    - LOF-adjustment on returns (Rugman & Verbeke) — foreignness cost
      per market discounts expected return

  Portfolio classification per market:

  STAR MARKETS          double-down; strong position + attractive growth
                        (CAGE + LOF-adjusted)
  CASH-COW MARKETS      hold; strong position but mature growth — fund other
                        markets from cash flow
  QUESTION-MARK MARKETS decide (invest more OR exit); weak position but growth
                        opportunity if invested
  DOG MARKETS           exit or minimum-viable-presence; weak position + low
                        growth


REGIONAL-VS-GLOBAL FRAMING (Rugman & Verbeke 2004)

  Most MNEs are REGIONAL not global. Rugman & Verbeke's research on Fortune
  Global 500: only ~10% had truly global sales distribution; ~80% were
  regionally concentrated.

  Portfolio implications:
    - Regional clustering value: neighboring markets share CAGE profile +
      allow shared resources (regional HQ / regional supply chain / regional
      talent pool)
    - Scattered-global reach is usually LESS value than regional density
    - Portfolio decisions should prefer regional consolidation to scattered
      expansion


PORTER COMPETITIVE POSITIONING (Porter 1980)

  Per-market Porter analysis for portfolio classification:
    - Competitive rivalry (concentration + differentiation of local competitors)
    - Buyer power + supplier power in market
    - Threat of substitutes + new entrants
    - Barriers to entry / exit per market

  Barriers-to-EXIT matter for divest decisions — high exit-barrier markets
  (long-lease commitments, WARN-Act-equivalent obligations, customer-
  transition duty) require longer divest timelines.


PORTFOLIO-MGMT OPERATIONAL SEQUENCE (this skill's phase-by-phase):

  Phase 1: PORTFOLIO INVENTORY                       (current markets + performance + resources)
  Phase 2: MARKET CLASSIFICATION                     (Ghemawat-adjusted BCG per market)
  Phase 3: CROSS-MARKET LEARNING TRANSFER SCOPING    (X market learning → Y, Z markets)
  Phase 4: REBALANCING DECISION                       (double-down / hold / invest / divest per market)
  Phase 5: MARKET-EXIT PROTOCOL                       (if any divest decision)
```

## Instructions

### Phase 1 — Portfolio inventory

Assemble per-market data:

- **Revenue + growth rate + margin** per market (cited from finance data)
- **Customer count + segment mix** per market
- **Resource allocation** — FTE / opex / capex per market
- **Time in market** (months / years since launch)
- **Original entry mode** (from `entry-mode-decision` output)
- **CAGE + LOF scores** (from `market-selection-framework` output at entry
  time — refresh if data has changed materially)
- **Regional grouping** (which markets cluster regionally)

### Phase 2 — Market classification (Ghemawat-adjusted BCG)

Per market, classify per BCG matrix with Ghemawat adjustments:

- Position axis: organizational position in the market (revenue rank / customer
  count / brand recognition) — 1-5 scale
- Growth axis: market growth rate × CAGE-distance factor (high-growth market
  with high CAGE distance discounted) — 1-5 scale
- Return adjustment: LOF-adjustment on returns

Plot on 2×2. Each market classified:

- **Star** (high position × high adjusted growth) → double-down candidate
- **Cash Cow** (high position × low adjusted growth) → hold; fund other markets
- **Question Mark** (low position × high adjusted growth) → invest-more OR exit
- **Dog** (low position × low adjusted growth) → exit or minimum-viable-
  presence

Overlay REGIONAL CLUSTERING per Rugman & Verbeke — mark markets that share
regional cluster. Regional Stars + Cash Cows together may support a regional-
HQ strategy.

### Phase 3 — Cross-market learning transfer scoping

For markets classified in similar quadrants + similar CAGE profile:

- **What worked in market X applicable to markets Y, Z?** — product features,
  pricing strategy, channel partnerships, marketing messages, hiring
  approaches, regulatory-navigation lessons
- **What DIDN'T work in market X, Y, Z with common cause?** — pattern
  identification prevents repeated mistakes
- **Cross-market operating rhythm** — regional teams / shared services /
  common talent pool

Document learning-transfer opportunities as concrete recommendations, not
generic "share best practices" advice.

### Phase 4 — Rebalancing decision per market

Per market, decision:

- **DOUBLE-DOWN** (Star markets, especially regional-cluster Stars) — increase
  resource allocation; invest in market position improvement; consider
  higher-commitment entry-mode upgrade (distributor → JV → greenfield if
  original entry was low-commitment)
- **HOLD** (Cash Cow markets) — maintain current resource allocation;
  optimize for margin + cash generation to fund other markets
- **INVEST MORE** (Question Marks with clear path to Star) — increase
  allocation with milestone gates
- **EXIT** (Dogs + Question Marks without clear path) — activate market-exit
  protocol (Phase 5)
- **MINIMUM-VIABLE-PRESENCE** (Dogs with strategic value beyond financial —
  brand-presence for global customers, learning-market, talent-access) —
  reduce allocation to minimum-viable

Decisions require cited per-market performance data + explicit rationale.
Silent drift = default failure mode.

### Phase 5 — Market-exit protocol (if any divest decision)

**EXIT is legally-adjacent + reputationally-sensitive.** LOAD-BEARING legal
fence + counsel involvement mandatory.

- **Employment-law compliance per jurisdiction** — LOAD-BEARING:
  - US: WARN Act 60-day notice for ≥100 employees
  - France: social plan (plan social) + works-council consultation
  - Germany: works-council consultation + social-plan negotiation
  - UK: collective consultation for ≥20 redundancies (30-day / 45-day
    minimums)
  - Coordinate with canopy `employment-law-multi-jurisdiction` + hire
    `payroll-and-eor` + operator + local employment counsel per jurisdiction
- **Customer-transition handling** — contracts, refunds, migration to
  alternative providers, communications
- **Regulatory close-out** — entity dissolution, tax final filings, license
  surrenders — coordinate with canopy `entity-setup-by-jurisdiction` +
  `tax-registration`
- **Brand-reputation impact** — coordinate with herald `press-kit` +
  `media-relations` for public communications
- **Investor comms** — Reg FD fence if material — coordinate with beacon
  `investor-cadence`
- **Employee comms** — coordinate with signal `change-comms` (with employment
  counsel involvement per LOAD-BEARING legal fence signal Principle 1
  inherited)
- **Timeline** — exits typically 6-18 months from decision to close depending
  on jurisdiction + entity complexity; do NOT commit to shorter timelines
  without counsel confirmation

## Output Format

Each invocation produces one or more of:

- **Portfolio inventory memo** — per-market performance + resources + regional
  grouping
- **Market classification matrix** — Ghemawat-adjusted BCG per market with
  regional-cluster overlay
- **Cross-market learning transfer memo** — concrete recommendations per
  transferable insight
- **Rebalancing decision memo** — double-down / hold / invest / divest /
  minimum-viable-presence per market with rationale
- **Market-exit protocol brief** (if any divest) — jurisdiction-specific
  employment-law + customer-transition + regulatory-close-out + brand +
  investor + employee comms coordination plan
- **Handoff briefs** to canopy + hire + signal + herald + beacon + operator +
  counsel

## Principles

1. **Annual portfolio review cadence.** Silent drift = default failure mode.
   Minimum yearly; more frequent for fast-changing environments.
2. **Ghemawat-adjusted BCG mandatory** — not financial-metrics-only. CAGE-
   adjusted growth + LOF-adjusted return + Porter competitive positioning.
3. **Regional-clustering respected** per Rugman & Verbeke. Regional density
   often > scattered global reach.
4. **Cross-market learning transfer documented explicitly.** Not "share best
   practices" generic; concrete per-insight recommendations.
5. **Divest decision LOAD-BEARING requires local employment counsel per
   jurisdiction.** WARN Act equivalents vary; skipping = predictable
   employment-law violation.
6. **Exit timeline COUNSEL-scoped.** 6-18 months typical; do NOT commit to
   shorter timelines without counsel confirmation.
7. **No fabrication** — cited per-market performance data + cited CAGE + LOF
   scores from prior compass work. Universal Principle 1.
8. **Aggregate-only at publication surface** — Universal Principle 2.
   Individual employee impact of exit decision aggregate-only in portfolio
   memo; individual-identifiable HR data routes through hire.
9. **Individual crisis HARD BOUNDARY** — Universal Principle 3 inherited.
10. **Legal fence** — Universal Principle 5. Divest decisions counsel-scoped
    per jurisdiction; investor comms Reg FD-fenced.
11. **§0.6 flag.** Ghemawat 2007 + 2011 + Rugman & Verbeke + Porter 1980 +
    BCG + MGI are Tier B. Downgrade to Tier A when Ghemawat 2007 + 2011 +
    Porter 1980 are placed and a `Shared OS/logical/expansion_portfolio.md`
    Route-D asset is built per §8.9.

## Fallback

- **Per-market performance data incomplete or stale.** Escalate to operator +
  CFO for data completion. Do NOT proceed to classification without data.
- **Regional-cluster overlap with adjacent orgs** (subsidiaries in same
  region; JV territories; distributor exclusive zones). Coordinate with
  operator + M&A/JV counsel to map cluster boundaries before classification.
- **Divest decision surfaces individual employee impact** — HARD BOUNDARY at
  individual-data level per Principle 8. Aggregate-only in portfolio memo;
  route individual HR handling through hire.
- **Divest counsel scoping delayed.** DEFER divest recommendation until
  counsel-scoped per jurisdiction. Do NOT commit to divest publicly without
  counsel confirmation of protocol.
- **Cross-venture portfolio decision** (multiple ventures with distinct
  market portfolios). Coordinate with marcus / vista for portfolio-of-
  ventures decision that upstream this skill.
- **Regulatory action forcing accelerated exit** (regulatory ban / sanctions
  imposition / regime change). Escalate to operator + international-trade
  counsel + relevant local counsel — regulatory-driven exits follow different
  protocol from strategic divests.
- **Investor pressure to exit specific market for financial reasons only.**
  Present Ghemawat-adjusted analysis showing strategic value beyond financial
  where applicable; escalate to operator + board for final call.
- **Individual crisis signal during portfolio conversation.** STOP. Route per
  Universal Principle 3 (inherited) to manager + HR Ops + EAP.
