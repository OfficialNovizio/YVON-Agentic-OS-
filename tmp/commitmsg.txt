   halves every chunk's score. Nothing populates quality_score (every row in the

   systematically dragging chunks under the threshold - measured 15 chunks
   'below reliability threshold' per turn. A never-measured metric is now
   neutral (1.0); only a real value discounts a chunk. Result: 15 -> 5-8.
