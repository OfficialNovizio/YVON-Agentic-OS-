Fix duplicated lifecycle events (204 emitted for one turn)

_emit_all looped over EVERY target actor, so a turn with three targets emitted
run.*, phase.*, gate.* and skill.* three times each - measured 204 ledger rows
for a single turn where ~68 were real. That triples Supabase writes, makes
'how many turns failed' unqueryable without dedup, and inflated the counts the
pipeline panel renders. Turn-scoped events are facts about the TURN, not about
each agent, so they are now emitted once and attributed to the primary actor.
Per-agent events keep the loop.

Also confirmed by live test (not by unit test): DeepSeek prompt caching works -
2nd call with an identical prefix returned cached_tokens 2688/2836 (95% hit) -
so the cacheRead:0 reported across a 24-call turn is a metering gap on the
streaming path, not a provider limitation. 1.8M input tokens at full price.
Not yet fixed.
