Give agents durable memory of what has already been gathered

Operator's question: once an agent scrapes/searches/gathers from the internet or
a graph, why can the next agent not access it - why redo it again and again?

There was no such memory. Audited state:
  - capture reuse in main.py is URL-keyed with a 12h TTL and covers ONLY the
    capture step, not terminal/read_file/web research an agent does itself;
  - turn artifacts land under <venture>/<correlation>/, and a NEW turn has a NEW
    correlation, so it cannot discover the previous turn's evidence;
  - the RAG index holds Teams/ documentation only - conversation artifacts never
    enter it;
  - MemPalace is write-only (drawers written, never queried).

New research_ledger.py - the missing READ path: append-only JSONL keyed by ROOM
(so a thread keeps its own context and other threads are not polluted), with a
400-record cap per room. Captures are recorded with their artifact paths and
measured digest, and prompt_block() injects a briefing into every subsequent
turn in that room telling the agent the evidence already exists, where it is, and
to gather again only for something materially NEW.

Contract: never raises; a missing or corrupt ledger returns empty and the turn
proceeds exactly as before.

Verified on the VPS: empty room -> '' (no block, unchanged behaviour); after two
records -> 617-char block naming the URL, the three artifact paths and the
measured digest.
