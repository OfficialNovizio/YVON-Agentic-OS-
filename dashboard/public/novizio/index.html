Atelier notes · Issue 12

The cutting room in October

Every Volume begins on a single roll of cloth. This season it is a compact merino from a family mill outside Braga — spun undyed, woven loosely, and left to relax for two weeks before the first pattern is laid on top of it.

“We would rather make eleven right pieces than thirty that merely photograph well.”

The result is a collection that barely raises its voice: twelve pieces, three palettes, one promise about how they age.
